# DEAI Checklist (Editor)

Use this checklist as part of the editor review. Flag any violation as a
blocking issue in the review report.

## Explanatory monologue

- [ ] No passage restates what the reader just witnessed.
- [ ] No character explains their own motivation aloud without reason.

## Formula repetition

- [ ] "Not X, but Y" / "Not just X, it was Y" appears at most once.
- [ ] No listicle rhythm (beat, beat, beat, summary).

## Emotional labels

- [ ] No "she felt sad" where an action or object would carry the beat.
- [ ] Emotion is shown through choice, body, or object, not label.

## Vocabulary

- [ ] No thesaurus inflation; words fit the voice anchors.
- [ ] No hollow metaphors (pretty but not load-bearing).

## Character shortcuts

- [ ] No demographic stereotype for minor characters.
- [ ] Each named character has at least one concrete, non-generic detail.

## Tone

- [ ] Intensity varies; not everything described at the same volume.
- [ ] Humour, if present, is load-bearing, not decorative.

## Subtext

- [ ] The chapter trusts the reader to infer at least one thing.
- [ ] No over-explanation that flattens the meaning.

## Verdict

- PASS: no blocking violations.
- FAIL: list each violation with its paragraph reference.
