## 关键事实
- # Summary: Can Floating Data Centres Meet AI's Huge Energy Demand?
- ## 🌍 The Core Challenge - **AI Power Demand:** Data centers already consume more electricity than some small nations.
- The **International Energy Agency (IEA)** projects demand will reach **945 TWh/year by 2030** (exceeding Japan's total consumption).
- - **Grid & Cooling Bottlenecks:** Land-based electrical grids are overwhelmed, and traditional data centers require massive water volumes for cooling.
