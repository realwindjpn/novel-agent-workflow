## 关键事实
- PEAC-seq: Prime Editor Assisted off-target Characterization
- Source:   Nature Communications  (2022)    DOI:  10.1038/s41467-022-35086-8
- CRISPR-Cas9 off-target edits and DNA translocations pose severe genotoxicity risks, especially for clinical translation. Existing detection methods (GUIDE-seq, DISCOVER-seq, CIRCLE-seq) suffer from:
- - Massive sequencing depth requirements (400–500M reads)
- Fusion Protein    Wild-type Cas9 fused to MMLV reverse transcriptase (replaces native Cas9n)
- Insertion Tag    21-nt sequence optimized to: (1) avoid RNA secondary structure, (2) ensure host-genome uniqueness, (3) enable efficient PCR annealing
- Fragmentation    Tn5 tagmentation (replaces sonication) with UMI-embedded adapters to eliminate PCR duplicates
- Primer Design    Forward/reverse primers target the tag at varying lengths to capture partial/full RT products.  Critical rule:  Primers placed ≥2 bp from insertion boundary to filter random priming artifacts
