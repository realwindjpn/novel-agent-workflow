# Concept Card

A concept card records a world-rule, technology, faction, or institution
that the story treats as canonical. It is the non-negotiable substrate the
reader must accept; contradictions are blocking errors unless given an
in-world reason.

## Definition

rule, state the rule. If it is a technology, state what it does and what it
cannot do. Avoid metaphor - this is the reference the editor checks against.
