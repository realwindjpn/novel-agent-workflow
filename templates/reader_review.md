# Reader Review

The reader reviewer reports the **real reading experience**. This is not
copy-editing. Provenance must differ from author and editor.

## Cold read

Read the chapter once, without notes. Then answer:

- Where did you have to re-read?
- Where did the world break (you noticed you were reading words)?
- Where did your attention drop?

## Over-explanation

- Did the chapter tell you what you already saw? Where?

## Subtext

- Did the chapter trust you to infer? Or did it flatten the meaning?

## End-state pull

- Did the ending make you want the next chapter? Why or why not?

## Verdict

- PASS: the chapter reads; no blocking experience problem.
- FAIL: cite the moment (paragraph/beat) of the blocking problem.
